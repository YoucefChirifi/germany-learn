📏 Pronunciation Rules to Remember

German Simple rule Example
W Sounds like English V Wasser (water)
V Often sounds like F Vater (father)
J Sounds like English Y Ja (yes)
Z Sounds like TS Zeit (time)
EI Sounds like English eye mein (my)
IE Sounds like a long “ee” Liebe (love)
SCH Sounds like English “sh” Schule (school)
CH Special German sound; varies by vowel ich (I)
